Selected projects

HERMES ↗ (project poster, PDF, opens in a new tab)

2026 · with Ento Industries

An automated counter for Black Soldier Fly neonates: deformable funnel, vibration-tuned ramp and an event-camera vision pipeline. 98.6% counting accuracy with 100% neonate survival, at S$1,120 a unit, under 1% of the cost of the commercial alternative.

Sprout ↗ (project slides, PDF, opens in a new tab)

2026 · SUTD 1D core project

A web platform that turns a garden visit into a collecting game. Scan or upload a plant photo, and a generative-AI pipeline identifies the species and draws a pixel-art “Plantemon” to collect and battle. Designed for families at Gardens by the Bay, it won the Dell Technologies Innovation Award.

Try the web app ↗

SensOri ↗ (project poster, PDF, opens in a new tab)

2026 · SUTD 3D design project

A real-time sensory map for autism-friendly outings. ESP32 nodes with radar, sound and light sensors score crowd density, noise and glare at each location, and a model forecasts the levels ahead, so caregivers can plan with confidence.

Interactive Assessment Device for ADL ↗ (project poster, PDF, opens in a new tab)

2021–22 · TP major project

A portable rehabilitation device that measures hand force and elbow motion as patients carry out everyday tasks, combining force sensors, LED-marker tracking and an augmented-reality game. Built on OpenCV, Pygame and SQLite, and filed as SG provisional patent 10202105829Q.

05 · Working with us
Capstone 2027/2028
No.PropertyValueRemark
01Participation feeS$6,000Per project team, including GST, paid by your organisation to SUTD
02SUTD project resources≤ S$4,000Provided by SUTD for the project’s allowable resources
03IP & generated materialsYoursAll IP belongs to the partner organisation
04Capstone period8 monthsAcademic terms 7 & 8, September 2027 to April 2028

We’re happy to do site visits and employee interviews, and to learn any specialised software the project needs.
Programme queries: capstone@sutd.edu.sg.

  1. Now – Jan 2027

    Scoping & introductions

    We shape a problem statement with you, then introduce you to SUTD’s Capstone Office, who will share the standard collaboration agreement.

  2. By 28 Feb 2027

    Project proposal

    We draft the project proposal with you and submit it to SUTD together.

  3. Feb – Mar 2027

    Scope review

    Your company mentor joins us for a session with SUTD’s Capstone Committee to agree the final scope.

  4. Apr – Jun 2027

    Agreement signed

    SUTD and your organisation sign the collaboration agreement, which confirms the project.

  5. Sep 2027 – Apr 2028

    The build

    Eight months on your problem, with design reviews along the way and a working deliverable at the end.
