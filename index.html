03 · Our partners

HERMES

2026 · with Ento Industries

An automated counter for Black Soldier Fly neonates: deformable funnel, vibration-tuned ramp and an event-camera vision pipeline. 98.6% counting accuracy with 100% neonate survival, at S$1,120 a unit — under 1% of the commercial alternative.

InVest

2026 · 3D design project

A smart navigation vest for the visually impaired: an ESP32-CAM captures bus numbers, an OCR pipeline reads them over wireless, and vibration motors steer the wearer and flag arrivals.

SensOri

2026 · 3D design project

A sensing system that lets caregivers plan outings with confidence — an ESP32 with radar, sound and light sensors monitoring crowd density, noise and glare in real time.

ADL Data Acquisition

2021–22 · TP major project

Computer-vision capture of activities of daily living for objective patient monitoring, built on OpenCV, Pygame and SQLite. Filed as SG provisional patent 10202105829Q.

  1. Now – Jan 2027

    Sourcing & introductions

    We connect you with SUTD’s Capstone Office by 29 January 2027; the company confirms agreement clauses and UEN by email.

  2. By 28 Feb 2027

    Project Form submitted

    Team and company complete the Project Form together — contract review typically needs at least a month before this date.

  3. Feb – Mar 2027

    Discussion session

    The Capstone Committee assesses project suitability; the initiating students and the company mentor attend.

  4. Apr – Jun 2027

    Agreement signed

    SUTD and the company sign the collaboration agreement — the project is approved once both signatures land.

  5. Sep 2027 – Apr 2028

    The build

    Two full terms on your problem, with design reviews along the way and a working deliverable at the end.
