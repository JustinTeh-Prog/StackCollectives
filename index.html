03 · Our partners

Organisations our members have interned with or built projects for.

Selected projects

HERMES

2026 · with Ento Industries

An automated counter for Black Soldier Fly neonates: deformable funnel, vibration-tuned ramp and an event-camera vision pipeline. 98.6% counting accuracy with 100% neonate survival, at S$1,120 a unit, under 1% of the cost of the commercial alternative.

InVest

2026 · 3D design project

A smart navigation vest for the visually impaired: an ESP32-CAM captures bus numbers, an OCR pipeline reads them over wireless, and vibration motors steer the wearer and flag arrivals.

SensOri

2026 · 3D design project

A sensing system that lets caregivers plan outings with confidence, using an ESP32 with radar, sound and light sensors monitoring crowd density, noise and glare in real time.

ADL Data Acquisition

2021–22 · TP major project

Computer-vision capture of activities of daily living for objective patient monitoring, built on OpenCV, Pygame and SQLite. Filed as SG provisional patent 10202105829Q.

We’re happy to do site visits and employee interviews, and to learn any specialised software the project needs.
Programme queries: capstone@sutd.edu.sg.

  1. Now – Jan 2027

    Scoping & introductions

    We shape a problem statement with you, then introduce you to SUTD’s Capstone Office, who will share the standard collaboration agreement.

  2. By 28 Feb 2027

    Project proposal

    We draft the project proposal with you and submit it to SUTD together.

  3. Feb – Mar 2027

    Scope review

    Your company mentor joins us for a session with SUTD’s Capstone Committee to agree the final scope.

  4. Apr – Jun 2027

    Agreement signed

    SUTD and your organisation sign the collaboration agreement, which confirms the project.

  5. Sep 2027 – Apr 2028

    The build

    Eight months on your problem, with design reviews along the way and a working deliverable at the end.
